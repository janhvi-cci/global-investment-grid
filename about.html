ABOUT GLOBAL INVESTMENT GRID

Connecting Global Capital with Global Opportunities

The Global Investment Grid (GIG) is an institutional portal designed to unify cross-border capital allocation by standardizing project discovery across countries, continents, and strategic economic sectors.

Our Institutional Purpose

International investment into critical infrastructure, clean energy transition assets, and advanced manufacturing has historically been fragmented across disparate municipal portals, sovereign promotion agencies, and ad-hoc bilateral delegations.

The Global Investment Grid addresses this market friction by establishing a single, standardized, and transparent gateway. Through GIG, sovereign wealth funds, multilateral development banks, pension managers, and private equity sponsors can review bankable project dossiers structured according to international due diligence standards.

How GIG Works

1. Standardized Project Pipeline

Projects listed on GIG adhere to rigorous classification standards, detailing development stage (Planning, Under Development, Implementation Ready, Operational Expansion), investment models (PPP, Greenfield, Brownfield, Joint Venture), and capital requirements.

2. For Institutional Investors

Allocators gain multi-parameter discovery tools across geographic jurisdictions and 16 strategic sectors, enabling granular filtering by ticket size, risk parameters, and offtake guarantees.

3. For Project Promoters

Statutory authorities, national investment promotion agencies (IPAs), and private lead developers gain direct visibility before an accredited global investor base, expediting expressions of interest and bilateral closing.

How Opportunities Are Discovered

Opportunities listed on GIG are sourced through three primary channels:

  • National Investment Promotion Agencies (IPAs): Sovereign statutory pipelines, national infrastructure priority lists, and Special Economic Zone tenders.
  • Multilateral Development Institutions: Concession projects prepared with technical assistance from the World Bank Group, Asian Development Bank, European Investment Bank, and African Development Bank.
  • Accredited Private Promoters: Private consortia seeking strategic co-equity, technology licensing partners, and long-term senior debt syndication.
